## What a contribution needs

See adding-heroes.md and CONTRIBUTING.md. Contribute reviewed original behavior, stable identity/parameter provenance and meaningful lifecycle/effect/replay tests; all four slots and the host consumer need acceptance before a whole hero enters the release profile. Do not unlock by changing a catalog boolean. New arbitrary IDs require a separate API/schema proposal.
